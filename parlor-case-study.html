01 — The Platform Decision

SHIP FAST.
FIGURE IT
OUT LATER.

The conventional wisdom says don't build on Wix if you're serious. The conventional wisdom hasn't tried to solo-found a feminist media platform while also editing, writing, designing, and running the business.

The Parlor needed to exist. Not in six months after a perfect technical spec — now. Wix offered something no other platform could at that moment: a full editorial CMS, hosting, payments infrastructure, and a serverless backend I could bend to my will, all without an engineering team.

Wix Velo — the platform's serverless layer — gives you backend JavaScript files that run server-side, HTTP functions that expose real API endpoints, a live database, and a secrets manager. It's comparable to Netlify Functions or Vercel Edge Functions. Most Wix users never touch it. I built my entire platform on top of it.

The workarounds became features. The constraints became architecture decisions. What started as a fast launch became a fully custom platform — one that no template could have produced.

Over time I built custom-coded elements across the site — authentication systems, subscription flows, paywalls, interactive embeds — using Velo to bridge gaps the platform wasn't designed to fill. Each constraint I engineered around is now a documented capability.

02 — Authentication Architecture

A LOGIN
SYSTEM BUILT
FROM SCRATCH

Wix provides authentication out of the box. What it doesn't provide is a system that bridges that authentication with a separate member portal, a living database, and persistent session state across custom-coded features. So I built one.

The authentication layer bridges Wix Auth with proprietary portal logic. When a member logs in, their session propagates through the portal, connects to the database, and determines what they can access across the platform based on their membership tier.

It's not a plugin. It's custom architecture that happens to run on Wix infrastructure — the same way complex applications run on AWS without being "an AWS product."

AUTH FLOW
  • →User login triggers Wix Auth session creation
  • →Custom Velo backend intercepts session, queries member database
  • →Member tier determined — subscriber, contributor, or community member
  • →Portal access granted based on tier — different views, features, and content for each
  • →Session state persists across portal navigation and all custom-coded components

03 — Content Architecture & Monetization Philosophy

THE JOURNALISM
IS ALWAYS
FREE.

The Parlor doesn't gate reporting behind money. What's behind the paywall is the magazine experience — the curated, designed, produced object. What's monetized in the free tier is depth and intimacy. This isn't just a pricing decision. It's a statement about what journalism should cost and what it shouldn't.

Tier 01
FREE EDITORIAL
Original reporting, essays, and data journalism. Always fully accessible. May include premium media accompaniments — custom audio and video players with microtransactions built in. The reporting is free. Going deeper costs.
Free · $2.50 per media piece
Tier 02
DIGITAL ISSUE
The same articles contained in the print magazine, presented as a fully designed digital experience. Always fully paywalled. Audio plays for 10 seconds, then the paywall interrupts both audio and scroll simultaneously.
Digital subscription · $2.50 single article
Tier 03
PRINT
The physical magazine. A flipbook preview lets readers browse before committing. Two purchase paths from inside the reader — recurring print subscription or single issue. Separate flow from digital entirely.

04 — The Digital Issue Paywall

10 SECONDS.
THEN THE
WALL HITS.

The digital issue paywall is a custom-coded interaction. When a reader lands on a digital issue article, the audio — many recorded by the writers themselves — begins playing. They can read and listen freely for 10 seconds.

Then the paywall fires. It doesn't just block the content — it interrupts the audio and locks the scroll simultaneously. Both sensory channels cut off at once. The effect is deliberate: the reader feels the absence of something they were already experiencing.

From the paywall, two paths forward: upgrade to a digital subscription, or purchase the single article for $2.50. Neither path redirects away from the page.

PAYWALL TRIGGER SEQUENCE
  • →Page load — article text visible, audio begins playing
  • →10 second timer fires — paywall overlay renders
  • →Audio interrupted and scroll locked simultaneously
  • →Two options presented — digital subscription or $2.50 single article
  • →On purchase — paywall dismisses, audio resumes, scroll unlocks in same session

The flipbook reader is a custom iframe element embedded directly in the site. It's not a third-party widget — it's a component I built and placed precisely within the editorial experience so that the magazine sells itself visually before asking for money.

The print flow is entirely separate from the digital issue flow. A print subscriber gets the physical magazine and portal access. A digital subscriber gets the digital issue. A single issue buyer gets the physical object. There is no crossover between the two checkout paths.

06 — Free Editorial + Premium Media

THE REPORTING
IS FREE.
DEPTH COSTS.

Free editorial content on The Parlor is never gated. But some articles carry something extra — a full interview, a recorded conversation, a video piece. These live alongside the free article as premium media accompaniments.

Custom-built audio and video players — currently in development — embed directly in the article with the microtransaction built into the player itself. A teaser plays. Then: listen to the full interview or watch the full conversation. One-time purchase or upgrade to digital.

Many of the audio pieces were recorded by the writers themselves. That intimacy — hearing the person who reported the story tell it in their own voice — is what has a price. Not the journalism. The experience of going deeper into it.

This is a different relationship with the paywall entirely. The reader never feels blocked from information. They feel invited to go further.

07 — Still Building

BUILT SOLO.
STILL BUILDING.

The Parlor is not a finished product. It's a living platform that evolves with the publication's needs and my own technical growth. Parts of the site are still under construction — intentionally. Each rebuild is better than the last.

The current migration from Airtable to Supabase is underway — moving the backend infrastructure to a more robust, scalable foundation while keeping the frontend experience continuous for existing members.
